# Third-party sources

The study uses CSAFE and ForensiCam imagery and published attribution and restoration methods: PRNU-Bench, wavelet/PCE, PCN, Restormer, BM3D and FBCNN. Access to the underlying datasets, software and trained weights follows the original providers' distribution terms and citation instructions.

This package contains recorded scalar research outputs and analysis/reproduction code. Python, NumPy and Matplotlib are external dependencies under their respective licenses.
